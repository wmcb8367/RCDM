The Process

The key to success is building a process for improvement.

Whether in school, business, relationships, or racing, top level performance comes from practice paired with a concrete process for learning.

McBride Racing uses sailing as the laboratory: training plans, decision frameworks, race reviews, and technical curriculum that help athletes learn faster and race with more confidence.
